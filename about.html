ABOUT YANGSUP / SEOUL

A little structure.

THINK / FEEL / MOVE
01 / THE STUDIO

Small team.
Close collaboration.

Yangsup is a three-person motion graphics agency founded in April 2025 in Mapo, Seoul. We work with artists, cultural organisations and public arts departments.

From the first conversation to the final frame, we stay involved. We translate a client’s intentions into geometric and abstract visual languages, then find the pace that makes them feel right.

02 / WHAT GUIDES US
01

Listen before drawing.

A good direction begins with the context: who is speaking, who is listening, and what needs to be felt.

02

Leave room for interpretation.

We use simple forms without flattening an idea. Abstraction lets a viewer bring something of their own.

03

Find the human rhythm.

Pauses, shifts and imperfect intervals give movement character. The right timing matters as much as the shape.

03 / IN PRACTICE

New tools.
Our own judgement.

We are experimenting with AI throughout our process. Our focus is on combining fast iteration with careful control of timing, nuance and each brand’s character. Designers shape the direction and review the result.
